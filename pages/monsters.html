QUARRY (SUPERNATURAL THREATS)/ - 5
QUARRY_01_VAMPIRES.DATVampires
Description: Information on timeless blood-drinkers hiding within human society, operating via shell companies, cults, and thralls. Known to feed on human blood, bend emotions, and masquerade as ordinary people ranging from bartenders to corporate executives.
Key Notes: Vulnerable to fire and staking; capable of turning victims into ghouls or new bloodsuckers.Group-
QUARRY_02_WEREWOLVES.DATWerewolves
Description: Ravening shapeshifters and apex predators enforcing a furious primitivism against human development and progress. Known to stalk wilderness areas, borderlands, and urban edges.
Key Notes: Highly volatile and capable of extreme physical violence.Group-
QUARRY_03_SORCERERS.DATSorcerers
Description: Warlocks, magi, and practitioners of esoteric rites who bend reality, weave curses, and perform blood or ritual magic with little regard for mortal collateral.
Key Notes: Often construct cults or employ enchanted techno-fetishes and minion networks to execute multi-stage schemes.Group-
QUARRY_04_GHOSTS.DATGhosts
Description: Restless apparitions and lingering spirits manifesting from beyond the veil of death to possess the living or haunt locations associated with their mortal pasts.
Key Notes: Often bound by unfinished business or past downfalls; require specialized exorcism or resolution tactics rather than conventional weapons.Group-
QUARRY_05_FAIR_FOLK_OTHERS.DATFair Folk & Others
Description: Enigmatic entities, otherworldly “good folk,” and anomalies operating at the forgotten margins of human perception and physical reality.
Key Notes: Includes liminal threats such as impossible spatial distortions and hand-drawn entryways that cause memory lapses or blackouts.Group-
ORGS (RIVAL ORGANIZATIONS & JOBBERS)/ - 8
ORG_01_SAD_FBI.DATSpecial Affairs Division (FBI)
Description: Special Affairs Division of the U.S. Federal Bureau of Investigation. A governmental agency investigating “unusual threats” and terrorism, often maintaining bureaucratic protocols and treating independent cells as rogue vigilantes or liabilities.Group-
ORG_02_IAO.DATInformation Awareness Office
Description: Information Awareness Office. A high-surveillance intelligence enterprise competing for jurisdiction over unusual threat investigations, data collection, and field ops.Group-
ORG_03_ST_LEOPOLD.DATSociety of St. Leopold
Description: Society of St. Leopold. A long-standing religious institution dedicated to purging monsters, primarily vampires, though driven by institutional dominance and internal hierarchy rather than individual Drive.Group-
ORG_04_ARCANUM.DATThe Arcanum
Description: The Arcanum. An organization focused on studying, cataloging, and archiving esoteric and occult lore, frequently prioritizing institutional acquisition over field intervention.Group-
ORG_05_MONSTER_X.DATMonster-X / Corporate UTR
Description: Monster-X, a privatized corporate monster-hunting contractor using corporate branding, NDA agreements, 401(k) benefits, and PR campaigns to profit from Unusual Threat Response.Group-
ORG_06_BOPE_RJ.DATBOPE-RJ (BOES of Rio de Janeiro)
Description: Specialized paramilitary police response units dealing with urban supernatural intrusions and heavy tactical operations.Group-
ORG_07_SPECIAL_REHAB.DATManila Department of Justice: Special Rehabilitation Program
Description: A state-sanctioned initiative addressing supernatural phenomena and compromised individuals under government oversight.Group-
